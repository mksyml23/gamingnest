About Neon Breach: Expansion (Idle Game)

Welcome to Neon Breach: Expansion, a deeply engaging cyberpunk incremental idle game available for free on GamingNest.nl. Start as a script kiddie and automate your way to becoming the architect of the universe by generating massive amounts of data!

Incremental Gameplay Features:

  • Automated Hardware: Upgrade your setup from simple Proxy Nodes and Botnets to Dyson Swarms and Reality Engines to boost your Bytes per second.
  • Software Protocols: Overclock your system to temporarily multiply your click power, or activate Breach protocols for massive production boosts.
  • Prestige System (Reboot): Reset your system to convert data into Cores. Use Cores to unlock the massive Kernel Skill Tree with permanent, game-changing multipliers.
  • Random Events: Catch golden and corrupted glitches on your screen for massive instant data rewards.

If you love idle games, clickers, and incremental strategy games with a dark synthwave aesthetic, Neon Breach is the perfect browser game for you. Keep the tab open to generate data while you are away!
