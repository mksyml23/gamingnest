About PixelStack Fever: Chaos Edition

Welcome to PixelStack Fever: Chaos Edition, a free online puzzle game on GamingNest.nl. If you love classic Tetris-style block-stacking games but crave more action, this game is for you!

Game Features & How to Play:

  • Pentominoes & Powerups: Stack blocks to clear lines and unleash bombs, freeze time, or trigger complete chaos.
  • Boss Battles: Clear enough lines to fight the 3D Boss Cube! Damage the boss by clearing lines before it fills your screen with garbage blocks.
  • Controls: Use the Arrow Keys to move blocks, Q/W or Up Arrow to rotate, and the Spacebar to hard drop.

Can you beat the Final Boss at Level 5 and survive the Mirror and Darkness dimensions? Play PixelStack Fever directly in your browser now!
