About Neon Parkour 3.0

Experience the ultimate freerunning challenge in Neon Parkour 3.0, a free 3D browser game exclusively on GamingNest.nl. Run, jump, and dash through 80 meticulously designed levels across 8 unique worlds, including the Lava Core, Sky Void, and the Laser Grid.

Game Features & Controls:

  • Fast-Paced 3D Platforming: Master your movement with momentum-based physics, jump pads, gravity zones, and an unlockable dash ability.
  • Global Leaderboards: Speedrun through levels and compete against other players worldwide for the fastest times in each sector.
  • Customize Your Runner: Collect hidden orbs to unlock exclusive skins like Cosmic, Midas Gold, and Stealth Black in the Gear Shop.
  • Controls: Use W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys to move, Spacebar to jump, Shift to sprint, and E to dash. Press V to toggle between 1st and 3rd person views!
