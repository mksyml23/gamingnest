🗺️ World Map & Guide

To escape the facility, you must survive the Containment Breach and reach the Surface.

🟩 Light Containment Zone

Threat: Low
Key SCPs: SCP-173, SCP-049-2
Goal: Find a Level 3 Keycard and reach the Checkpoint.

🟥 Heavy Containment Zone

Threat: Extreme
Key SCPs: SCP-049, SCP-096, SCP-106
Goal: Survive and locate the Entrance Zone Checkpoint.

🟨 Entrance Zone

Threat: High
Key Enemies: Chaos Insurgency
Goal: Find Gate A or Gate B to escape.

🟦 Surface Zone

Threat: Unknown
Key SCPs: SCP-682
Goal: Reach the extraction point alive.

Optional Areas (Loot & XP)

  • 🏢 Abandoned Offices: Beware of SCP-049-2 instances.
  • 🧪 Testing Chambers: Find hidden supplies and keycards.
  • ☢️ Reactor Room: High risk, high reward area.

About SCP Foundation: Containment Breach RPG

Welcome to SCP Foundation: Containment Breach, a free text-based RPG available on GamingNest.nl. Step into the shoes of a D-Class, MTF Guard, or Researcher as you try to survive a catastrophic containment failure at Site-19.

Features of this Text Adventure Game:

  • Encounter dangerous anomalies: Face off against legendary SCPs like SCP-173 (The Sculpture), SCP-096 (Shy Guy), SCP-106, and the indestructible SCP-682.
  • RPG Mechanics: Upgrade your STR, DEF, MAG, and AGI. Manage your HP and collect vital loot like Level 5 Keycards, Medkits, and the Micro H.I.D.
  • Multiple Endings: Will you escape through Gate A, activate the Alpha Warhead, or be rescued by the Chaos Insurgency? Your choices matter.

Play this immersive SCP survival game completely for free in your browser right now, only on GamingNest!
