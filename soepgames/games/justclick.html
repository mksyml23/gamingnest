About Just Click: The Grind

Welcome to Just Click: The Grind, an addictive clicker and incremental game available for free on GamingNest.nl. Test your Click Per Second (CPS) speed, earn Pixels, and grind your way to the top of the global leaderboards!

Game Features:

  • Click Speed Tests: Challenge yourself in 1s, 3s, 5s, 10s, 30s, 60s, or Random time modes to set new high scores.
  • Progression System: Gain XP with every click, level up from Novice to Transcendent, and unlock exclusive achievements.
  • Pixel Shop & Cosmetics: Spend your hard-earned Pixels on custom profile pictures (PFPs), unique cursors, accessories, and rare legendary titles to stand out on the leaderboard.
  • Daily Rewards & Events: Spin the Daily Wheel for bonus XP and Pixels, and take advantage of weekend multipliers!
